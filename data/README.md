# Data

Everything in this folder is synthetic. No real claim, policyholder, insurer, or company data was used to produce any of it.

## synthetic_claims.csv

200 fabricated first-notification and adjuster-style claim notes, generated by `generate_synthetic_claims.py` from templates and word lists with a fixed random seed. The generator is committed so provenance is demonstrable and so you can regenerate or extend the set (`python data/generate_synthetic_claims.py --n 500`).

Columns: `claim_id`, `note`, `category`.

### Labelling rules

A classification task is only as defensible as its label definitions. The gold labels follow these rules, and any evaluation of model output should be read against them:

| Label | Rule |
|---|---|
| `MOTOR_BI` | Motor incident where any person is injured or reports symptoms, however minor, whenever reported. Injury takes precedence over damage. |
| `MOTOR_PD` | Motor incident with vehicle or property damage only. Explicit confirmation of no injury, or no injury indicated. |
| `PROPERTY_WATER` | Escape of water or internal leak damage at the insured property, regardless of origin (own or neighbouring premises). |
| `PROPERTY_FIRE` | Fire, smoke, or fire-suppression damage. Origin governs: water damage caused by extinguishing a fire is a fire loss. |
| `LIABILITY_INJURY` | Third-party injury on or arising from the insured premises or operations. |

Roughly a fifth of the notes are deliberately hard: injury emerging after a damage-led motor note, flood water inside a motor claim, sprinkler water damage inside a fire loss, smoke-only damage, property repair costs inside a liability claim. This is what real notes look like, and it is why the examples validate model output rather than trusting it.

## sample_methodology.md

A fabricated term assurance valuation methodology document (~1,300 words, ten sections) used as the corpus for the document Q&A example. It is written to be internally coherent and realistic in register, but every figure, threshold, and policy in it is invented. It is flagged as illustrative in the document itself.
